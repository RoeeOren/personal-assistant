# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 5 October 2026**

## Who this applies to

This application is a personal tool used by one person — its author — on a single personal computer. It has no other users and is not distributed.

## What data is accessed

With the author's own Google account, and only after the author grants consent, the application reads:

- Gmail messages and their metadata, using the `gmail.readonly` scope

That scope is read-only. The application cannot send, modify, or delete mail.

## How the data is used

Messages are stored in a database on the author's own computer so they can be searched. The data is used for no other purpose.

## Where the data goes

Nowhere. The data is not transmitted to any server, not shared with any third party, not sold, and not used for advertising, analytics, or model training. It remains on one local machine, on an encrypted disk.

## Retention and deletion

Data is kept locally for as long as the author finds it useful, and is deleted by deleting the local database. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately stops any further reading.
